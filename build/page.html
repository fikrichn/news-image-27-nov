WELLINGTON · POLITICS · THE FINAL COUNT

65–65: New Zealand's final count deadlocks parliament — a brand-new party holds the keys

The Electoral Commission declared the official results of the 7 November election on Friday, and after 484,865 special votes the governing centre-right coalition fell one seat short: National (40), ACT (13) and New Zealand First (12) hold 65 of 130 seats, with 66 needed to govern. Labour won the party vote, 29.88% to 28.35%, and 36 seats; the left bloc (Greens 17, Te Pāti Māori 5) holds 58 — and even with help it reaches only 65. Between the blocs sit the seven seats of Opportunity, a centrist party led by 38-year-old Qiulae Wong: only her seats can push a bloc past 66, making her the kingmaker PM Christopher Luxon must win over.

THE HAGUE · JUSTICE

Duterte's ICC trial opens Monday — families demand justice

Former Philippine president Rodrigo Duterte, 81, goes on trial at the International Criminal Court on Monday, as families of those killed in his drug war still seek justice. He faces allegations over 85 murders and two attempted murders from 2011 to 2018. Duterte will be asked to plead on Monday — a trial that will also test the court itself.

TASMANIA · FIRE

Flinders Island fire still burning: 13,000 hectares gone

The lightning-sparked bushfire on Flinders Island in Bass Strait was still out of control on Friday, with about 13,000 hectares burned. Emergency warnings remained in place around Mount Boyes and a Watch & Act covered Palana. Earlier in the week nine people — plus a dog and a wombat — were taken off West End Beach by boat.

MARKETS · FRIDAY IN ASIA

Shares steady after a bruising month; dollar reigns

Asian shares steadied on Friday as a holiday lull calmed AI jitters: the Nikkei rose about 0.7%, South Korean shares rallied 2% and the Asia-Pacific index added 0.5% — still down 8.2% for November. BRUSSELS · EU SUMMIT

No deal yet: EU budget talks run on into December

EU leaders' talks on the bloc's €1.78 trillion 2028–2034 budget continued on Friday with no agreement. A proposed loan for Ukraine drew the support of 24 member states. Council president Costa urged leaders to show the willingness to negotiate that a timely deal will need.
